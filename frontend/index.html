      About this data

      Timetables come from the public DataMeet Indian Railways dataset (CC0), a historical snapshot from around 2016-17. Some trains, timings and station codes have changed since (for example, Mumbai CST appears as CSTM). Fares are estimates. The delay-risk model is trained on synthetic labels, so it is a demonstration, not a forecast. Always confirm on official channels before travelling.
